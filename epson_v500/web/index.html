V500 Scanner

Epson Perfection V500 Photo, driven from the browser over WebUSB. No Epson software involved.

This browser has no WebUSB. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer, and open the page from http://localhost or over https.

Not connected

2 Firmware

The scanner needs its firmware after every power-on. Pick Epson's Linux driver download (iscan-gt-x770-bundle-*.tar.gz, from Epson's support site); the page takes the firmware out of it and keeps it in this browser.

Templates (optional)

Captures of Epson's driver (tools/capture) add resolutions the page can't build yet.
